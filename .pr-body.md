## Summary

Merges `dev` into `main` for production release. This PR includes **Epic 13** (Account Dashboard), **Epic 14** (Self-Serve Account Deletion), auth UX improvements, and assorted docs and infra fixes.

---

## Epic 13 — Account Dashboard (v4.5.0)

- **Account dashboard page** (`/account`): email display, password change form, placeholder delete section
- **Interactive account menu**: sidebar footer converted to dropdown with "Manage Account" and "Sign Out"
- **Server actions**: `signOutAction()`, `changePassword()` with current-password verification and session invalidation via `passwordChangedAt`
- **Fix**: Sign out immediately after password change (Epic 13 follow-up)

---

## Epic 14 — Self-Serve Account Deletion (v4.6.0)

- **Self-serve deletion**: Users can permanently delete their account from the dashboard
- **Hard-delete strategy**: `prisma.account.delete()` with full cascade (User, Forms, Fields, Submissions, PasswordResetTokens); removes `deletedAt` tombstone and related edge cases
- **Confirmation dialog**: Destructive styling, clear warning; post-deletion sign-out and redirect to login with "Your account has been deleted." banner
- **Docs**: `docs/ACCOUNT_OPERATIONS.md`, epic plan, CHANGELOG
